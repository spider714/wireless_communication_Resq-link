How this system works

RESQ LINK is an emergency response system. It works even without mobile network or internet, by using long-range radio to send alerts from remote areas to a control point.

1Alert sent — a device sends a distress signal over long-range radio (LoRa).
2Signal received — the receiver picks up the signal and sends it to this dashboard.
3Alert sorted — the system checks the alert type and finds distance and time to reach.
4Help notified — the alert details can be sent to police, ambulance, fire or rescue teams.

Where devices can be placed

Devices can be placed in remote areas without needing mobile towers, wires or main power.

Forests & trailsFixed on a pole with a simple SOS button.
Riverbanks & flood areasFitted with a water-level sensor that sends an alert on its own.
Villages & schoolsSet up as a public help-point for medical, fire or police emergencies.
Device details
  • Weatherproof box, safe for rain and sun.
  • Runs on solar power with battery backup, works for weeks without sunlight.
  • Long-range antenna for better signal reach.
  • Built with ESP32 and LoRa radio module, plus GPS for location.

How responders use it

Fire BrigadeGets the exact location and type of fire danger.
AmbulanceGets patient details, distance and route to reach fast.
PoliceGets alerts for safety threats and SOS signals.
